About

I'm Sarkis — an EECS student at UC Berkeley who loves turning ideas into working software.

I co-founded PathToPi, a nonprofit that's raised $50k and built 70+ chapters with 120+ mentors to make tech education accessible. I've interned as a software engineer, competed in cybersecurity at the national level, and taught CS to underserved students in Armenia.

My work spans React/TypeScript web apps, machine learning with scikit-learn, PowerShell automation, and mobile development with React Native. I care deeply about building things that make a real difference in people's lives.
